## Overview

- **Safety First:** No injection. Not an unlocker. Operating since WoW 11.0 with no detection incidents or account bans reported in our team's records to date *(note: no third-party tool can guarantee zero future risk; avoid arena automation where player reports can increase risk)*.
- **Supported Game Version:** **WoW Retail `12.1.5` (Midnight)** on Windows 10 / 11.
- **Not Supported:** Classic, PLUS, and WoW Forever *(Beta/PTR support follows official announcements)*.
- **Included in Every Rotation:** Automatic interrupts, ongoing patch updates, and anti-detection protection.
- **Free Experience:** New accounts include **24 hours of Basic and Healing access**. Paid 1-day trials (including fine-tuned specializations) are also available in the client — once per specialization per account.

## Specialization Coverage

### DPS & Tanks — All 13 Classes
Death Knight · Demon Hunter · Druid · Evoker · Hunter · Mage · Monk · Paladin · Priest · Rogue · Shaman · Warlock · Warrior

- SimC-based single-target and AoE combat rotations, with a force single-target toggle
- Separate controls for burst cooldowns, trinkets, and combat potions
- Automatic self-healing and defensive abilities tailored to your specialization

More details: [wowrotationbot.com/dps-rotations/](https://wowrotationbot.com/dps-rotations/)

### Healers — All 7 Healing Specializations
Restoration Druid · Preservation Evoker · Mistweaver Monk · Holy Paladin · Discipline Priest · Holy Priest · Restoration Shaman

- Chooses healing targets and spells based on party or raid health
- Automatic dispels with a quick on/off toggle when an encounter requires it
- Dedicated **Pre-heal mode** for Restoration Druid, Mistweaver Monk, and Discipline Priest

More details: [wowrotationbot.com/healing/](https://wowrotationbot.com/healing/)
