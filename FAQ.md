# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Official site: [https://wowrotationbot.com/](https://wowrotationbot.com/) · Support: [Discord](https://discord.gg/NNACX7tp8)

### Which World of Warcraft versions are supported?
- **Supported:** World of Warcraft Retail **12.1.5 (Midnight)** on Windows 10 / 11.
- **Not supported:** WoW Classic, PLUS, and WoW Forever. Beta/PTR support follows announcements in Discord.

### Is it safe? Does it inject into the game or act as an unlocker?
- **No injection. Not an unlocker.**
- We have operated since WoW 11.0. According to our team’s records, no detection incidents or account bans have been reported to date. However, no third-party tool can guarantee zero future risk. Avoid arena automation, where player reports can increase account risk.

### Can I try it before buying a full plan?
- Yes. New accounts include **24 hours of Basic and Healing access** free of charge.
- In addition, 1-day trials (including fine-tuned DPS/tank specializations) are available inside the client — once per specialization per account.

### Which classes and healing specs are supported?
- **All 13 WoW Retail classes** (all DPS and tank specializations).
- **All 7 healing specializations:** Restoration Druid, Preservation Evoker, Mistweaver Monk, Holy Paladin, Discipline Priest, Holy Priest, and Restoration Shaman.
- Dedicated **Pre-heal mode** is available for Restoration Druid, Mistweaver Monk, and Discipline Priest.

### Where can I get help or report an issue?
- Join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/NNACX7tp8) for setup help, free registration instructions, and ongoing patch announcements.
